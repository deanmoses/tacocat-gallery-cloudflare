# Risks

Risks that could stop pix.tacocat.com moving from AWS to Cloudflare, and what's been done to mitigate. Timings are [Globalping](https://globalping.io) probes from Paris, Marseille, Baton Rouge and Los Angeles, and `api/scripts/probe.sh` from Berkeley, with the D1 primary in SJC and read replication on. "Worker" is time spent inside the Worker; TTFB adds the visitor's connection. How performance is judged against the AWS site, and every measurement behind it, is in `docs/Perf.md`.

Status: ✅ solved, ⚠️ solved with a catch, ❌ fails today, ⏳ open or untested.

## Performance

| #   | Risk                                                                                             | Status | Result                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| --- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1   | Reads from an active regional replica are not under 100 ms from California, Louisiana and France | ✅     | Album reads take 17 to 70 ms in the Worker (TTFB 41 to 130 ms). A replica is only active under steady traffic, which this site does not have; see #2.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| 2   | Reads after hours of idle are not under 100 ms                                                   | ❌     | Outside California. After 4 and 8 idle hours, the first album read from Paris and Baton Rouge went to the SJC primary every time: 338 to 631 ms in the Worker from Paris, 67 to 301 ms from Baton Rouge, against 17 to 43 ms in California. Repeat reads seconds later from Paris mostly stayed on the primary too, at 173 to 194 ms. The WEUR and ENAM replicas are inactive at this traffic. Three runs so far.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| 3   | Derived images are not faster than the AWS image CDN                                             | ❌     | Stored derivatives cached per colo through the Cache API take 17 to 66 ms warm. A colo's first request goes to R2 in WNAM, 250 to 550 ms from Baton Rouge and Paris, and at this traffic that is the usual case; an R2 custom domain with Tiered Cache was no faster. The AWS image CDN's misses take 271 ms at the median and 388 ms at p90. Album JSON is not edge-cached for the same reason: in a week of the AWS site's logs, most album views were the first of that page at that edge, a 35 to 40% hit rate (`docs/plans/EdgeCachedAlbums.md` in `tacocat-gallery-sam`). Next: a second derived bucket in WEUR, whose cold read from Paris is unmeasured. |
| 4   | The page and JS chunks are slow from a cold location                                             | ⏳     | The asset router serves them without running the Worker, and nothing has requested them from Paris or Baton Rouge, so where a cold request is answered is unmeasured. On AWS, CloudFront edges keep immutable chunks for hours to days.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |

## Web app

| 5   | SvelteKit SSR on the Worker is awkward                         | ❌     | Not until SvelteKit 3. | 6   | SvelteKit as a static single-page app on the Worker is awkward | ✅     | One Worker serves `web/`'s static build and the API on `pix.deanmoses.com`: `/` and `/2001/06-15` get the app's `index.html` from the asset router without reaching the Worker, `/login` and `/api/*` reach the Worker. | 7   | Read-your-writes after an admin save is slow or wrong            | ✅     | Reads through the Sessions API bookmark see the admin's write, from replicas too. ## Media

| #   | Risk                                                                         | Status | Result                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| --- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| 11  | Images binding cannot reproduce the crop-then-cover thumbnails               | ✅     | With a change: `fit: cover` centres and upscales, unlike SAM's Sharp code, so the Worker computes SAM's source rectangle and output size and asks Images only to `trim` and resize. `trim` coordinates are in the EXIF-oriented frame.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| 12  | Images binding mishandles EXIF orientation or HEIC                           | ❌     | EXIF orientation works, and so does 8-bit iPhone HEIC, which is every one of 863 sampled from 2023 to 2026. 10-bit HEIC and some non-iPhone HEIC fail (errors 9412, 9516). Planned fallback, not built: `heif-dec` in the existing container writes one full-size JPEG, and every size is derived from that. Some files named `.heic` are JPEGs, so uploads must sniff content. `GET /debug/image/<r2 key>` reproduces the failures.                                           |
| 13  | Images binding bills something at zero usage, or needs a paid plan           | ⏳     | No charge or subscription has shown up so far; an invoice will settle it.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| 14  | R2 event notification to Queue to Worker is not a usable upload pipeline     | ⚠️     | An upload is processed within seconds. The consumer is idempotent (the version id comes from the event, the row is written before the original, the inbox object is deleted last), failures land in a dead-letter queue, and a rejected file becomes a row in `upload_error`, read with `POST /api/errors`. Only S3 API writes raise the event: a file written through the Cloudflare REST API, including `wrangler r2 object put` and the dashboard, is never processed.      |
| 15  | Browser presigned PUT to R2 fails on CORS or needs a custom domain           | ✅     | The browser PUTs straight to R2 with a URL the Worker signs, given the bucket CORS rule in `api/r2-cors.json`.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| 16  | Video: Stream cannot take iPhone HEVC, or an ffmpeg Container is too awkward | ⚠️     | ffmpeg in a Container turns iPhone HDR HEVC into a 1080p H.264 MP4 and a poster. `standard-2` is the production size: a 10 s 4K HDR clip takes about 35 s end to end and 0.2% of the monthly container allowance. Output is capped at 1080p because the tone-map dominates the time. The tone-map (hable, `desat=0`) is watchable but warmer and redder than the phone's own rendering. A file ffmpeg rejects becomes an upload error rather than a retry. Stream is untested. |
| 17  | Upload metadata extraction differs from Lambda                               | ❌     | ExifReader reads EXIF and IPTC in a Worker, but XMP needs `DOMParser`, which Workers lack, so XMP fields are not read until an XML parser is passed to ExifReader.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |

## Admin

| #   | Risk                                           | Status | Result                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| --- | ---------------------------------------------- | ------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| 18  | Passkey-only admin login is too much machinery | ✅     | Works, on an iPhone too: `/login`, `/invite/<token>` and a signed 30-day session cookie. A passkey is bound to the hostname, so an `Origin` allowlist picks the site, and `wrangler dev` needs `--local-upstream`. A login challenge lets someone in once: a D1 row records it until its cookie expires, since synced passkeys report a sign count of 0 and the count cannot catch a replay. A session cannot be revoked early: removing an admin means deleting their passkeys and rotating `SESSION_SECRET`, which logs every admin out. |

## Platform and migration

| #   | Risk                                                            | Status | Result                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| --- | --------------------------------------------------------------- | ------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 19  | Free plan fails closed                                          | ⚠️     | Production needs Workers Paid ($5/month). On Workers Free, past 5M D1 rows read in a UTC day every query in the account fails until midnight.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| 20  | Three environments and CI cannot be driven from config          | ⏳     | `infra/` (OpenTofu) declares the zone, bot and cache settings, D1, the buckets, the R2 custom domain, the queue and its notification; `api/wrangler.jsonc` owns the Worker, bindings, routes and crons, and the D1 schema ships as migrations. Provider gaps: no per-category AI crawler policies; R2 custom domains and event notifications cannot be imported; importing D1 needs `ignore_changes` on the location hint. One account API token has to be made by hand, since Wrangler's OAuth login stops at `zone:read`. Not built: environments, previews, deploy on push (the planned release is under Infrastructure in `README.md`). Unverified: whether a Worker holding a Durable Object gets preview URLs. |
| 21  | Moving tacocat.com's nameservers to Cloudflare breaks something | ⏳     | Untried; `pix.deanmoses.com` stands in. The zone is at DreamHost with GoDaddy as registrar: 52 records, about 35 after dropping leftovers, no DNSSEC, with Google mail the part needing care (`docs/plans/HostingDeepDive.md` in `tacocat-gallery-sam`). Some networks cache DNS for hours after a nameserver move; expect it for some visitors.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| 22  | Migrating the real gallery is slow or costly                    | ⏳     | Untested. Production holds 37,875 media items in 1,843 albums and 135 GB of originals. Originals have to reach R2 through its S3 API, or a backfill has to queue them, since other writes never reach the upload pipeline (#14). Derived images: the Images binding includes 5,000 unique transformations a month, and two variants of each of about 38,000 images is about 75,000, so a backfill is billed beyond the allowance and not yet priced.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| 23  | No backup of the originals outside Cloudflare                   | ⏳     | R2 has no object versioning. An upload never overwrites an original, since each gets its own version id, but nothing guards against deletion or losing the account. On AWS, AWS Backup covers this (weekly, 35-day retention, $6.75 a month). The D1 dump (#10) is in the same account too. Options are in _Replacing S3 versioning_ in `docs/plans/HostingDeepDive.md` in `tacocat-gallery-sam`.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
