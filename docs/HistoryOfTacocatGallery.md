# History of the Tacocat Gallery

The gallery's photos have been held by seven systems in 25 years, and each move left something behind. Until 2013 each system also made the pages readers saw; from 2013 an app in the browser, written by Moses, read the gallery as JSON from an API in front of whatever held it, so the app was rewritten every few years while the photos stayed where they were. This is what each era was, when, and where its remains are, as worked out in September and October 2026 from what is still at DreamHost, the Internet Archive's index of `tacocat.com`, Moses' GitHub, his Dropbox, his emails and his memory. What each move lost, and what comes back, is in [Recovering lost content from pre-AWS galleries](plans/RecoverPreAwsGalleries.md).

A row starts whenever what held the photos or what readers saw changed. Paths starting `~/` are on DreamHost, and repos are Moses' on GitHub, `github.com/deanmoses/`.

## Before the gallery

`tacocat.com` began as Moses' personal site: the Internet Archive has its pages from 1998, guides to ActiveX, pages on CNET and New York, and pages about Lucie and friends. Photos went up from 1999 as bare folders, `tacocat.com/pix/1999-06/`, `tacocat.com/pix/1999-10/halloween/`, which the web server listed as it lists any folder, with no pages or captions. They are at DreamHost as `~/tacocat.com/pix/1999/06/` and `~/tacocat.com/pix/1999/10/`, and most are in the gallery's 1999 albums.

## LView Pro: December 2001 to 2004

The gallery proper began with Felix, born in December 2001. Each week got an album, its title the week of his life, "Week 22, May 7-13, 2002", and its folder named for the week's dates, `tacocat.com/pix/2001.12.17-23/`, `tacocat.com/pix/2002.4.2-4.15/`. Each album led back to "the main Felix page", `tacocat.com/felix/`. LView Pro, a Windows image editor, made each album with its Web Gallery feature: an album page with the week's story and a table of thumbnails, `index.htm`; a page per photo with its caption and Previous, Up and Next buttons, in `html/`; the photos at 640 pixels in `images/`; and thumbnails in `thumbnails/`. Every page carries its `<meta name="generator" content="LView(R) Pro Web Gallery">`.

From May 2002 a new camera took video. No version of the gallery played video until AWS in January 2026, so Moses cut each clip down to a small MPEG, uploaded it to a `video/` folder beside the album and linked it from the album's story: "See this week's videos." The camera's originals went into Dropbox's `Photos/raw/…/not_for_tacocat/video/` folders, `not_for_tacocat` being what was not published to the site.

## JAlbum: 2005 to 2006

From 2005 the albums were made with JAlbum, a Java desktop program, which turns a folder of photos into a web album through a skin of templates. The pages name no generator, but their layout is JAlbum's, an `index.html` with `slides/`, `thumbs/` and `res/` folders, and they keep its templates' comments, such as "Include header.inc from the source image directory if present" and "Image, maybe with link to original", which JAlbum's skin guide quotes. Their look and CSS classes, `thumbnail-cell`, `returntoindex-text`, are no stock skin's, so the skin was most likely Moses' own. Each year had a page of its albums, `tacocat.com/pix/2005/index.php`, and the albums had an RSS feed through FeedBurner.

## Gallery 2: January 2007 to 2014

On 2007-01-09 the gallery moved to Gallery 2, Menalto's PHP and MySQL gallery, at `tacocat.com/pictures/`, version 2.3.1 by the end. Gallery 2 took its first albums that day, and the same day `pix/` was rearranged: the weekly folders became `tacocat.com/pix/YYYY/MM/DD/` and the JAlbum albums were regenerated, which is why every file in `~/tacocat.com/pix/` is dated 2007-01-09. The static albums stayed up beside Gallery 2, which held the albums from 2007 on. Its albums were day folders, `2008/01-13/`, with sub-albums inside some, `2009/05-17/yosemite/`, and most photos were uploaded as copies 1024 or 640 pixels on the long side. The Everybody group's view permission said what the public saw. Gallery 2 made its pages from PHP templates, whose customizations are in [tacocat-gallery-php](https://github.com/deanmoses/tacocat-gallery-php). `tacocat.com/pix/index.html` forwarded readers to the current year, `tacocat.com/pictures/v/2013/`, later `/pictures/v/2014/`.

In 2013 the server started handing out data instead of pages. [tacocat-gallery-php-json](https://github.com/deanmoses/tacocat-gallery-php-json), a Gallery 2 plugin of February 2013, gave Gallery 2 a JSON API, and in September 2013 [tacocat-gallery-backbone2](https://github.com/deanmoses/tacocat-gallery-backbone2), an app in Backbone, went to production at `tacocat.com/p/`. It read Gallery 2's albums through the plugin and the albums before 2007 from the JSON that [tacocat-gallery-albums](https://github.com/deanmoses/tacocat-gallery-albums) scraped from the static pages into [tacocat-gallery-data](https://github.com/deanmoses/tacocat-gallery-data), so one front end showed the whole gallery a year before one system held it. By the end of that month it logged in and created albums. The home page still sent readers to Gallery 2's pages, so the app may have been Moses' own way in.

## Zenphoto: November 2014 to 2023

On 2014-11-23 the gallery started moving to Zenphoto, version 1.6 by the end, at `tacocat.com/zenphoto/`. Gallery 2's albums were copied over in November and December 2014, its sub-albums becoming day albums of their own, `2009/05-17/yosemite/` becoming `2009/05-16/`, and the full-size originals replacing Gallery 2's copies. The albums before 2007, back to scans of 1918, came into Zenphoto too, so for the first time one system held the whole gallery. Zenphoto marked unpublished albums as hidden, which is how drafts were kept. Zenphoto's own pages were for running it; readers saw it only through Moses' apps.

The move brought [tacocat-gallery-zenphoto-rest-api](https://github.com/deanmoses/tacocat-gallery-zenphoto-rest-api), a Zenphoto plugin, and [tacocat-gallery-react-ampersand](https://github.com/deanmoses/tacocat-gallery-react-ampersand), an app in React and Ampersand, first deployed to `tacocat.com/p2/` on 2014-12-03 and moved to `tacocat.com/p/` on 2014-12-14, `p2/` staying its staging. By January 2015 the home page's "fresh photos" linked `tacocat.com/p/`. The album was in the address's fragment, and Moses' emails link the gallery as `tacocat.com/p/#2001/12-31` from March 2015 to early 2023.

In January 2018 [tacocat-gallery-redux](https://github.com/deanmoses/tacocat-gallery-redux), in React, Redux and TypeScript, replaced it at `tacocat.com/p/`, with `tacocat.com/p3/` its staging, and also answered at `pix.tacocat.com`, with the same fragment addresses. The plugin was rewritten as [zenphoto-json-rest-api](https://github.com/deanmoses/zenphoto-json-rest-api), for any Zenphoto, and [tacocat-gallery-json-filecache](https://github.com/deanmoses/tacocat-gallery-json-filecache) and `~/tacocat.com/p_json/` kept albums' JSON as files on disk, `p_json/` as a file per year.

[tacocat-gallery-sveltekit](https://github.com/deanmoses/tacocat-gallery-sveltekit) was begun in December 2021 and ran on `tacocat.com/p3/` through 2022. At the turn of 2023 it went to production at `pix.tacocat.com`, served from `~/prod-pix.tacocat.com/` with `~/dev-pix.tacocat.com/` its staging, still reading Zenphoto. It gave each album a real path, `pix.tacocat.com/2001/12-31`, and sent the old fragment addresses there.

## AWS: December 2023 to 2026

On 2023-12-13 the files were copied from Zenphoto into S3 and every published album and photo written to DynamoDB, and the gallery moved to `pix.tacocat.com` on AWS: the SvelteKit app on S3 and CloudFront, reading the API of [tacocat-gallery-sam](https://github.com/deanmoses/tacocat-gallery-sam), Lambdas behind API Gateway, with Cognito for login and Redis for search, from four repos. The drafts were left behind. Video arrived in January 2026, transcoded by MediaConvert. DreamHost stopped hosting `prod-pix.tacocat.com` and `dev-pix.tacocat.com` on 2026-10-02.

## Cloudflare: October 2026

This repo, where one Worker serves the SvelteKit app, now in `web/`, and its API, filled from AWS by [Migrating the AWS gallery data](plans/migration_from_aws/AwsDataMigration.md) and from what the earlier moves left behind by [Recovering lost content from pre-AWS galleries](plans/RecoverPreAwsGalleries.md).

## Tried and dropped

None of these served readers.

- **Galleries:** Gallery 3, in the database `tacocat_gallery3`, and Piwigo, at `themosii.com`, each tried for a day in September 2013, and Piwigo once more on 2014-11-23, the day the move to Zenphoto began.
- **Front ends:** Backbone in [tacocat-gallery-backbone](https://github.com/deanmoses/tacocat-gallery-backbone) and [tacocat-gallery-bbb](https://github.com/deanmoses/tacocat-gallery-bbb) in 2013, before `tacocat-gallery-backbone2`; [tacocat-gallery-angular](https://github.com/deanmoses/tacocat-gallery-angular) and [tacocat-gallery-react-backbone](https://github.com/deanmoses/tacocat-gallery-react-backbone) in 2014; and [tacocat-gallery-web-components](https://github.com/deanmoses/tacocat-gallery-web-components), [tacocat-gallery-svelte](https://github.com/deanmoses/tacocat-gallery-svelte) and [tacocat-gallery-flexbox](https://github.com/deanmoses/tacocat-gallery-flexbox), a week of trials in December 2021 before SvelteKit.
- **Back ends:** [tacocat-gallery-flask](https://github.com/deanmoses/tacocat-gallery-flask), a REST service in Flask, in 2013, and [tacocat-gallery-aws](https://github.com/deanmoses/tacocat-gallery-aws), a serverless back end with image recognition from 2018 to 2020, which nothing shows went live.
